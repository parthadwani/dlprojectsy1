info About The Hackathon

Hack-JECRC 3.0 represents Rajasthan’s premier student-led innovation cauldron. Over an intense 36-hour continuous sprint inside our flagship Central Computing Complex, university developers, designers, and innovators synthesize scalable prototypes solving high-stakes institutional, societal, and technological friction points. Participants gain 24x7 hardware access, high-speed fiber uplinks, peer collaboration lounges, and uninhibited advisory sessions with lead engineers from industry frontrunners.

Official Hackathon Rules & Integrity Code

  • check_circle Fresh Code Mandate: All lines of application source code must be written within the designated 36-hour sprint. Open-source libraries and APIs are permitted provided they are documented.
  • check_circle Team Composition: Teams must strictly maintain 2 to 4 registered members. Cross-college and interdisciplinary collaborations are fully permissible.
  • check_circle Physical Presence: At least 2 team members must report on-campus to Audi-2 for the final live judging evaluation round.
  • check_circle Zero Tolerance Plagiarism: Automated GitHub classroom commit timeline audits will be executed before final shortlist announcements.

Is lodging and food provided for outside participants?

Yes. All non-local shortlisted teams will receive hostel accommodation inside the JECRC campus along with all 6 meals, refreshments, and midnight snacks at no extra cost.

Can we change our team members or problem track later?

You can edit your roster and selected track up to 24 hours prior to the event start via the Organizer Studio portal.

What hardware components are provided by JECRC?

Teams registered for the IoT & Hardware track can request ESP32 modules, sensor kits, breadboards, and soldering stations at the equipment counter.
